# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept feedback gracefully, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads and block people who break these rules. Report a problem to info@elmeris.com with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports are handled privately.
