# Getting help

- **How do I...?** Read the reference in [docs/](docs/) and the examples in [examples/](examples/), then ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/elmerisllc/terraform-provider-algizian/discussions).
- **Something does not work.** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/elmerisllc/terraform-provider-algizian/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include the provider and Terraform versions, the configuration (without secrets) and the error.
- **I need a new resource or argument.** Open a [feature request](https://github.com/elmerisllc/terraform-provider-algizian/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security problem.** Do not open an issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Account, billing or the Algizian service itself.** Contact us through [algizian.com](https://algizian.com).

Never paste tokens, client secrets, provider secrets or state files into an issue.
